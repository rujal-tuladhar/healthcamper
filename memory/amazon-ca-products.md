## Amazon Associates Canada rules that matter for the site

I couldn't open associates.amazon.ca from this session. The points below come from secondary sources and Amazon's US help pages, so confirm them in the Canadian Operating Agreement and Program Policies before going live.

1. **Disclosure.** The site must show Amazon's required statement, or something substantially similar. The standard wording is "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases." Put it next to the shelf, along with the site's own plain-language line ("We earn a small commission if you buy through these links"). Canada's Competition Act also expects clear, nearby disclosure. Source: policy summary at conductatlas.com. The agreement is at https://associates.amazon.ca/gp/associates/agreement.
2. **Prices.** Program Policies say a site may show prices or availability only if (a) Amazon serves the link or widget that shows them, or (b) the data comes from the Product Advertising API or Creators API. API prices also need a date/time stamp next to them. **So the prices in this file must not be typed into the site as static text.** The safe options are to show no price ("See price on Amazon.ca") or to use Amazon-served links. Sources: https://conductatlas.com/platform/amazon-associates/amazon-associates-program-policies/provision/CA-P-064489/price-display-requires-api-feed-or-amazon-served-link/ and https://conductatlas.com/platform/amazon-associates/amazon-associates-program-policies/provision/CA-P-064575/price-display-via-data-feeds-requires-timestamp-disclosure/
3. **Images.** Amazon product images may only be used through approved tools: PA-API/Creators API, or SiteStripe image links served by Amazon. Downloading them and hosting them in the repo is not allowed (per affiliate-compliance guides, which are secondary sources). The safe option for a static site is text cards with our own illustrations, or SiteStripe image links once there is an account. One third-party source says PA-API was deprecated in May 2026 in favour of the "Creators API". This is not confirmed; check Amazon's docs. PA-API access also usually requires an account with qualifying sales.
4. **Tracking tag.** Every link needs the owner's Associates tag (for example `https://www.amazon.ca/dp/B092DXFGVH?tag=XXXX-20`). Rujal has no account yet, so the links above are clean and untagged and would earn nothing. Per CLAUDE.md, an amazon.ca account must make 3 qualifying sales within 180 days or it is closed. Signing up is Rujal's decision; nothing was signed up or submitted.
5. **Other points.** Don't say "Amazon's price" or compare it with other stores. Links must point straight to amazon.ca (link shorteners and cloaking are restricted). Don't put affiliate links in emails or PDFs. Keep product claims modest, following the brand's no-medical-claims rule.
