### Avoid for now: DSers/AliExpress-style dropshipping
Delivery from China takes 1–3+ weeks. The **US duty-free de minimis exemption is suspended for all countries** [26]. The EU is ending its €150 customs-duty exemption from 1 Jul 2026, with a €3/item flat duty [27]. GPSR requires an EU responsible person and product information [28]. Quality and safety are unknown, and it clashes with a "calm, trustworthy" brand.

## 2. Digital content (printable journals, breathing PDFs, sound packs)
| App | Fees | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Shopify Digital Downloads** (by Shopify) | **Free** [29] | Basic: attach files to products and email a link. Enough to start. |
| **Sky Pilot** | Free plan (~100 MB storage ⚠️); paid tiers by storage/bandwidth ⚠️ [29] | Streaming, a download portal, and good handling of big audio files, which suits sound packs. |
| **SendOwl** | ~$18/mo ⚠️ (one source says $4.99) [29] | Licence keys, drip content, upsells. |

Physical shipping doesn't apply, but digital sales carry the **heaviest tax burden** (see section 3).
- **EU:** non-EU sellers owe VAT from the first sale. The €10k threshold applies only to EU-based businesses ⚠️ (conflicting sources [30]).
- **Canada:** GST/HST is due once Rujal passes $30k worldwide taxable sales [31].
- **Japan:** foreign businesses register only above ¥10M [32].
- **UK:** VAT is due from the first digital sale ⚠️.

**Practical answer:** sell digital products only to **Canada (and possibly the US)** at first. Alternatively, sell them through a merchant-of-record platform (⚠️ e.g. Lemon Squeezy, Gumroad, Payhip handle EU/UK VAT for you) and link out from Shopify.

## 3. Cross-border must-knows (Canadian sole proprietor)

- **EU GPSR (in force since 13 Dec 2024).** Consumer products sold into the EU need an **EU-established responsible economic operator**: an EU manufacturer, importer, authorised representative or **fulfilment provider** (GPSR Art. 16, referring to Art. 4 of Reg. 2019/1020; article numbering is cited inconsistently ⚠️) [28]. Under Art. 19, each online listing must show the manufacturer, the responsible person, product identifiers, and warnings/safety information in the **local language**. There is no small-seller exemption [28].
  - POD items printed inside the EU by Gelato or Printful may let the printer act as the fulfilment provider. ⚠️ Confirm each vendor's GPSR offer before selling.
- **EU VAT.**
  - **Goods ≤€150** shipped from outside the EU: register for **IOSS** (needs an EU intermediary for non-EU sellers ⚠️) or the customer pays VAT plus fees on delivery.
  - **Goods printed inside the EU** count as EU distance sales. The usual approach is to register for Union OSS through an EU VAT number ⚠️. Gelato and Printful each describe their own VAT handling, so check it.
  - **Digital products:** VAT is due via **non-Union OSS** (one quarterly return) ⚠️ from the first sale [30].
  - **Customs duty:** the €150 duty exemption ends **1 Jul 2026**, replaced by a €3/item flat duty until 2028 [27].
- **UK.**
  - Goods **≤£135** sold from abroad directly to UK consumers: the overseas seller **must register for UK VAT, with no threshold** [33].
  - Goods printed inside the UK (e.g. by Printful UK): ⚠️ UK VAT registration is likely still required because Rujal is a non-established business with no threshold.
  - EU GPSR does **not** apply in Great Britain (UK GPSR 2005 rules apply), but it **does** apply in Northern Ireland ⚠️.
- **Japan.**
  - Consumption tax is 10%. Foreign sellers of digital services register only above **¥10M** in taxable sales [32].
  - Low-value imports up to ¥10,000 are currently exempt. The exemption is proposed to end **April 2028** (Diet approval not confirmed) [34].
  - Customers expect Japanese-language listings, sizing, care labels and support. Locally printed Gelato or Printful orders avoid import charges [24].
- **US.**
  - The **$800 de minimis is suspended for all countries.** Executive Order 14388 (20 Feb 2026) continued it, and CBP rules dated 24 Jun 2026 made it indefinite, including postal shipments [26].
  - Canada→US parcels now face duties unless goods qualify under CUSMA with origin paperwork. Canada Post uses a third-party duty processor [35].
  - **Fulfil US orders from US facilities**, not from Toronto.
  - Sales-tax economic nexus is mostly **$100k per state**. 16+ states have dropped the 200-transaction test. CA and others use $500k [36].
- **Canada.**
  - GST/HST registration is required after **$30k** in taxable sales over a calendar quarter or four consecutive quarters (this covers digital products sold to Canadians) [31].
  - ⚠️ Exports of goods, and digital products sold to non-residents, are generally zero-rated.
- **Shopify Markets / Managed Markets.**
  - Markets (multi-currency and multi-language) is included in Shopify plans.
  - **Managed Markets** (Global-e as merchant of record, which handles duties, VAT and IOSS) **opened to Canadian and UK merchants on 15 Jul 2026** [37].
  - Fees are unclear ⚠️: reported ~3.5% extra per order plus 1.5% currency conversion, versus 6.5% in the earlier US rollout [37].
  - ⚠️ Check whether Managed Markets works with **POD/dropship apps** and **digital products**. It historically excluded some product types.
  - Managed Markets is the simplest way to stay VAT/duty-compliant in the UK, EU and Japan without registering in each country.
- **Health and wellness claims.**
  - **FTC (US):** health claims need competent, reliable scientific evidence, usually human RCTs. This applies to "health equipment" and apps too [38].
  - **UK ASA/CAP:** rules out anxiety, mood and treatment claims for non-medical products. A new Therapeutic and Health Advertising Code applies from 1 Apr / 1 Jul 2026 [39][40].
  - **For Health Camper:** use comfort and ritual language ("a cosy blanket for quiet evenings"). Never write "reduces anxiety", "improves sleep" or "for depression". This matches the existing CLAUDE.md shop rules.

## 4. Recommended phased plan

**Phase 1: Canada + US (now).**
- **Printful** covers branded items: Toronto for Canada; Charlotte and Dallas for the US. It is free, already planned, and has no customs between countries.
- Add **Shopify Digital Downloads** (free) for PDFs and sound packs, selling to Canada only, or Canada + US. US states rarely tax digital goods below the nexus thresholds ⚠️.
- Gear: start with the **affiliate shelf** (MEC/Amazon) that's already planned. Optionally test 2–3 small, non-regulated items (string lights ⚠️ electrical certification, sleep masks, camp pillows) from **CJ's or Spocket's US warehouses**.
- Avoid tents and sleeping bags.

**Phase 2: UK + Japan (after ~3 months of steady CA/US sales).**
- Add the **Gelato** app alongside Printful. It produces locally in Japan, the UK, and the EU, which covers the most G7 countries from one app.
- Or use Printful UK + Printful Japan if the product range there is enough.
- Prefer turning on **Managed Markets** to handle UK VAT and Japanese consumption tax, instead of registering directly. Confirm fees and POD compatibility first.
- Translate the Japan storefront into Japanese with Shopify's translation features, and get the copy human-checked.
- Keep digital products **off** these markets, or sell them through a merchant-of-record platform.

**Phase 3: EU (France, Germany, Italy), delayed.**
- POD only, via **Gelato** (local FR/DE/IT production) or **Printful** EU (Spain/Latvia).
- Do this only once these are in place:
  1. a confirmed **GPSR responsible person** (the printer's offer or a paid EU representative, ⚠️ roughly €100s/yr)
  2. listings with safety information in French, German and Italian
  3. a VAT solution (Managed Markets, or OSS)
- **Skip general gear in the EU** (BigBuy/CJ EU) until revenue justifies the GPSR and compliance work.

**What to delay or avoid everywhere:**
- AliExpress/DSers
- tents, sleeping bags, fuel and candles (regulated or dangerous goods)
- Japanese domestic wholesale dropshipping
- EU and UK digital sales without a merchant of record

**Cheapest stack covering all 7 countries for branded goods:** Printful (CA/US/UK/EU/JP) + Gelato (as a backup and for local FR/DE/IT/JP) + Shopify Digital Downloads. Total $0/month in app fees. Pay per order only.
